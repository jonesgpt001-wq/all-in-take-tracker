Method

  • Source. Episode audio from the public All-In RSS feed, transcribed with speaker attribution from conversation cues. Every prediction links the episode and approximate timestamp.
  • AI commentary. The per-host profiles and trend commentary are AI-generated from the measured grades and always labeled; the grades, quotes and sources underneath are the receipts.
  • Extraction. Only forward-looking, falsifiable claims count. Analysis, hedging and vibes are out. Boldness (distance from consensus at the time) and importance are scored 1-5 when the prediction is logged and never edited afterward.
  • Grading. Each call is resolved against the public record: correct, partial, wrong. Long-horizon calls sit as pending. A declined prediction is shown but not scored.
  • Score. 100 x SUM(accuracy x weight) / SUM(weight). No recency decay in the pilot - all calls are from 2020.
